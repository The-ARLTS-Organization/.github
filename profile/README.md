# The ARLTS Organization

**A free, non-profit English-learning platform:** [arlts.org](https://arlts.org)

ARLTS is a free, non-profit English-learning platform for people who learn with a teacher, a tutor, or a course. It covers the groundwork, vocabulary and grammar, and the time with a teacher can then go to using English, not relearning words.

Its practice activities, among them word recognition, gap-fill, error correction, dictation, and reading, run from CEFR A2 to C1 and follow the Oxford 3000 and 5000 word lists. The Test Center checks a learner's level, and the Library keeps the words a learner wants to review.

There is no login wall on the learning path, no ads, and no paid tier. ARLTS is run by The ARLTS Organization, part of The Phan Initiatives, in Ho Chi Minh City.
