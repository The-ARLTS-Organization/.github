# How to report a problem with ARLTS

If you find a weakness in arlts.org or in the ARLTS PDF Guard, please write to **contact@arlts.org**, in English or Vietnamese. Tell us what you found, where, and how to repeat it.

Please:

- Never look at, change, or keep a learner's data. Use your own test account if you need one.
- Don't run heavy or automated tests against arlts.org. It runs on free plans, and a flood of requests can take it offline for learners.
- Give us time to fix it before you tell anyone else.

We read every report and reply. Our protection work is open: [arlts.org/protection](https://arlts.org/protection), and [our contact file](https://arlts.org/.well-known/security.txt) has that email too.

Thank you for helping keep ARLTS a place where people can learn in peace.
